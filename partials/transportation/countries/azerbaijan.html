Azerbaijan Transportation Strategy

How locals move — cheapest by day, safest by night.

🌞 Daytime Mobility

  • Default mode: Baku Metro
  • Cheapest mode: Bus (BakuBus)
  • Recommended card: BakiKart
Purchase a BakiKart at metro station kiosks before boarding.

🌙 Nighttime Mobility

  • Safest mode: Ride-hailing
  • Recommended apps: Bolt, Yango
Bolt is widely used in Baku with transparent fixed pricing.

📱 Recommended Apps

  • Ride-hailing: Bolt, Yango, Uber AZ
  • Transit: 2GIS, Google Maps

🚆 Intercity Travel

  • Cheapest: Marshrutka / Bus
  • Fastest: ADY High-Speed Train
  • Recommended: ADY Train (Baku to Gabala/Ganja)
