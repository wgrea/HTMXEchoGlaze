🚗 Transportation Strategy

How locals move — cheapest by day, safest by night.

🌞 Daytime Mobility

  • Default mode: Subte
  • Cheapest mode: Colectivo (bus)
  • Recommended card: SUBE
The SUBE card is mandatory and sometimes hard to find—buy it at a "Kiosco".

🌙 Nighttime Mobility

  • Safest mode: Ride-hailing
  • Recommended apps: Cabify, Uber
Cabify is often preferred by locals for safety and fixed pricing.

📱 Recommended Apps

  • Ride-hailing: Cabify, Uber, Didi
  • Transit: Moovit, Google Maps
  • Navigation: Google Maps

🚆 Intercity Travel

  • Cheapest: Bus
  • Fastest: Plane
  • Recommended: Plane (Aerolineas Argentinas)
